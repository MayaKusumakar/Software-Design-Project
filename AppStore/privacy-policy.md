# Maybe Calendar Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 14, 2026*

**Maybe Calendar does not collect any data about you.**

## What the app stores, and where
- Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. That includes your plans, your ideas, their categories, and which suggestions you added or said "Not now" to.
- Smart Suggestions are worked out on your iPhone. Nothing about your plans or ideas is sent anywhere.

## What the app does not do
- No account or sign-in.
- No servers. The app does not connect to the internet.
- No analytics, no ads, and no tracking.
- No access to your contacts, location, photos, or the iPhone's own Calendar app.

## Backups
If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, the app's data is included in that backup like any other app's data. That backup is handled by Apple under Apple's terms. The developer cannot see it.

## Deleting your data
Delete the app from your iPhone. Its data is deleted with it.

## Children
The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If a future version ever changes this (for example, adding sync between devices), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
