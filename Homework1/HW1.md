## **Which app are u cloning and why?**

I chose to clone the calendar app. The calendar app is an app I personally use every day, and it is the biggest tool that helps me keep track of my schedule. Sometimes, I have a lot of potential plans or ideas that I might want to do, but I don’t want to fully commit to them. I want a calendar that helps me visualize and list these possible plans.

## **What problem does this solve?**

This app helps people organize their schedule, free time, and keep track of commitments and possible alternative plans. A lot of times, people commit to plans and forget, double book two commitments, or have to many plans to remember them all in the first place. The calendar app solves this problem by keeping track of the user’s commitments and visualizing the user’s schedule. My version of this app will focus specifically on keeping track of possible plans as well as still keeping the main function of the calendar app: to organize someone’s schedule. Being able to keep track of undecided commitments will help people remember plans that they might want to do, while also keeping track of prior commitments. 

## **What are you going to keep, change, or remove:**

### **Keep:** 

A basic calendar view with the option to add, edit, and delete events 
Events will still keep titles, date, times, and notes

### **Change:**

Events can be marked as “potential” or “confirmed”. Possible plans will show up on the calendar as a “seperate layer”. This layer will be able to be turned on and off. When it is turned on, tentative plans will show up in a more transparent color and a dashed border. There will be a plus icon to easily add the plan to the calendar and commit to it. 

### **Add:**
I want to add an ideas list for activities that the user wants to do when they have time, but it does not have a date, and is more of a free time activity.
Users can move these ideas onto the calendar easily when they want to do said activity. The user will be able to mark this activity plan as potential or confirmed as well.

### **Remove:**
* Shared calendars
* Invitations
* Other integrated services like teams, google meets, etc
* Recurring events (maybe)
* Multiple different accounts on calendar
* Tasks or Todos
* Other complicated features that take away from the main calendar idea

## **Overall:**

The main focus of my app will be to act as a personal calendar where users can keep track of their schedule as well as possible plans that they might want to do. Often, I find myself forgetting plans when I don’t commit to them and add them to my calendar, but I also don’t want to block off that time if something more important comes up. My app aims to solve this problem.
